# Zomato-Sales-Analysis
Zomato Data Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)
Detailed Project Description

The Zomato Data Analysis Dashboard is an end-to-end data analytics project built using Power BI, aimed at extracting meaningful insights from restaurant data. This project focuses on analyzing customer preferences, restaurant distribution, pricing patterns, and service availability to help understand trends in the food industry.

The dashboard transforms raw data into an interactive and visually rich report, enabling users to explore key business metrics through filters and dynamic visuals. It is designed to simulate real-world business intelligence scenarios where decision-makers rely on dashboards to identify opportunities and improve performance.

🎯 Objectives
To analyze restaurant data and identify customer behavior patterns
To evaluate restaurant performance based on ratings and votes
To understand city-wise and cuisine-wise distribution
To explore pricing trends and affordability
To provide insights into online delivery and service availability
📌 Key Insights Derived
⭐ Ratings Analysis
Majority of restaurants fall under the “Good” rating category (~57%)
A smaller percentage falls under Average (~28%)
Some restaurants remain Not Rated (~14%), indicating potential gaps in customer engagement
📍 City-wise Distribution
Gurgaon has the highest concentration of restaurants (~42%)
New Delhi and Noida follow with equal distribution (~28% each)
Indicates strong food business presence in NCR regions
🍴 Cuisine Analysis
Fast Food dominates the market (~42%)
Followed by Chinese and Mexican cuisines (~28% each)
Other cuisines like Bakery, Cafe, Tea, Continental have moderate representation
💰 Cost Analysis
The maximum average cost for two is around ₹900
Most restaurants fall into affordable to mid-range pricing categories
🗳️ Votes Analysis
High number of votes indicates active customer engagement
“Good” rated restaurants receive the majority of votes
🚚 Service Availability
Most restaurants offer online delivery
Delivery filters help analyze operational reach and customer convenience
